About StreamRadar

Find titles, compare streaming offers, and keep a watchlist.

Your library

Your ratings and watchlist are saved on this browser. Use Export backup to keep a copy or move to another device. Clearing site data removes local saves. Existing records from this browser are imported when available; new saves are not sent through the old unauthenticated cloud connection.

Originals

All streaming services default to Originals only. Choose Full catalog to include licensed titles. Series use original-network affiliations; movies require first US digital-release evidence naming the service or a matching official title page with no earlier wide theatrical release. Upcoming movies can use official service title pages. Metadata is incomplete and may omit originals. The All service combines supported services' originals; Theaters keeps its theatrical scope.

Reality and talk shows

All genres includes reality, competition and talk shows. Choose Reality & talk in the Genre dropdown to browse only those series. Categories follow TMDB genre metadata; documentaries and stand-up remain available.

Discovery preferences

Hide watched removes Seen It titles from browsing, search, and actor filmographies. Your Seen It library remains accessible. Recommendations always exclude watched titles. Viewer-score filters use TMDB community scores with at least 20 votes. Mood filters use genre groupings. Runtime limits exclude titles with unknown runtimes; series use the listed episode runtime. Recommendations verify US subscription offers for your selected services. Preferences are saved on this browser.

Data sources

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

The Movie Database supplies titles, cast, keywords and related-title recommendations.

Streaming availability is provided by JustWatch via TMDB. Subscription, ad-supported, free, rent and buy offers are shown separately. Availability may differ by plan and region.

IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic scores are supplied by MDBList, not live connections to each rating site. Retrieval times do not guarantee the upstream data was updated at that time.

Stand-up labels use TMDB keywords or explicit stand-up/special titles. Missing labels mean unclassified, not necessarily “not stand-up.” Calendar dates refer to original releases or episodes, not a guaranteed streaming arrival.
